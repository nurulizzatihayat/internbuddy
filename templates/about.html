What is Intern Buddy?

Internship hunting can get messy fast

Intern Buddy helps students stay organised during their internship search.

Discover opportunities, track your applications, and keep everything you need in one place.

Application

Keep your internship applications organised in one place. Track companies, roles, application statuses, deadlines, and important notes without relying on scattered spreadsheets or documents.

Discover

Explore internship opportunities shared by other students. Discover companies and roles you may not have considered and get a broader view of the opportunities available to you.

Progress

Stay aware of where you are in your internship search. Monitor your applications from the initial listing through interviews, offers, and final outcomes, giving you a clearer picture of your overall progress.
